Five ways to draw FaiND.

Same palette, same typeface (Poppins Medium, the one the current wordmark is drawn in), five different ideas for the mark. Each is shown on light and dark, as an app icon, and at 16 to 48 pixels, where most logos fail.

The nucleusEvery mark shares one atom: the gold core. On light it sits inside a deep steel ring, because gold on mist is under 3 to 1.
FaiNDF, N and D in ink; "ai" in steel on light and gold on dark. Screen readers get "FaiND" from the title.
MethodMade with the logo-generator skill (op7418): one or two elements, 40% or more empty space, strokes thick enough to survive a favicon.
01

Found dot

Punto encontrado

The direct evolution of today's mark. The gold dot leaves the centre and rises, like the dot of an i finding its place. In the wordmark that same dot becomes the dot on the i of FaiND.

Verdict. Lowest risk. Keeps today's equity and adds a story: the dot that found its place.

02

Hireframe

Hireframe

Four wireframe corners, like the handles of a selection in Figma, frame a gold core. It joins the repo name (hire + wireframe) with the idea of choosing what matters.

Verdict. Speaks to designers first. The only proposal that names the repo visually.

SVG: wordmark light · dark · mark light · dark · icon light · dark

03

Open lens

Lupa abierta

An almost closed ring is the search. Where the handle would be there is a gold core: the search ends in a find. It reads as a lens without being the generic search icon.

Verdict. Clearest at 16 px. The meaning (search, then find) reads at a glance.

SVG: wordmark light · dark · mark light · dark · icon light · dark

04

One in many

Uno entre muchos

A grid of 16 quiet tiles and one gold one. It is about honest numbers: many applications, one role. No drama.

Verdict. Most on-voice for the honest numbers. Busier than the others below 24 px.

SVG: wordmark light · dark · mark light · dark · icon light · dark

05

Steady route

Ruta tranquila

A soft S path from a hollow start to the core. This is the Keep your head part: the search as a route you can see, at a steady pace.

Verdict. Warmest and most human. Ties to the scroll lines on the content pages.
