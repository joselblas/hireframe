# hireframe brand

The live version, with downloads, is at [design/brand/](https://joselblas.github.io/hireframe/design/brand/).

## The idea

Find a job. Keep your head. hireframe is a job search kit for designers: hire plus wireframe. The AI does the typing, but it is not the headline. The strongest parts of the product are privacy, plain-text context and honest numbers, and the AI serves those.

## The name

- Write it **hireframe**, all lower case, also at the start of a sentence. Say it "hire-frame".
- It is a designer's pun: hire plus wireframe. Designers get it at once, and it is the same name as the repository, the website and the skills, so there is only one thing to remember and search for.
- Never publish the name alone. The first time on a page, in a title or in a post, add what it is: "hireframe, the AI job search kit for designers". Search engines read "hireframe" as "wireframe", so the description is what makes it findable.
- The repository was called `jobops-kit` until November 2026, and the product was briefly called FaiND. Both names are retired. Old GitHub links redirect. The skills are `hireframe-start` and the others (renamed from `jobops-` and then `faind-` on 18 November 2026); people with older skills installed remove them and add the new folders.

### Name risk

Why not FaiND: searching the web on 13 November 2026 found several products with the same or a very close name (an AI app called FAIND, two different Faind products, and a company spelled exactly FaiND), nobody would type it as written, and some screen readers spell a word with capitals in the middle letter by letter.

hireframe, checked on 18 November 2026: search engines treat it as "wireframe", and the closest name found was Shireframe, an older open-source wireframing tool for programmers, not a job search product. I have not checked domain names, social handles or the EUIPO trademark register. The mitigation is the rule above: always pair the name with its description.

## Wordmark

The Hireframe mark and the name, drawn as outlines so they look the same everywhere. Four wireframe corners, like the handles of a selection in a design tool, frame one gold core: hire plus wireframe, and choosing what matters. "hire" is in the accent colour. The type is Poppins Medium, all lower case. Chosen on 18 November 2026 from five proposals in `design/brand/proposals/`.

- On light, the gold core sits inside a deep steel ring (`#2E5C72`), because gold on mist is under 3 to 1. On dark and on deep ink the core stands alone.

- Use the supplied files in `design/brand/assets/`. Light version on light, dark version on dark.
- Clear space: three times the width of the gold core. Minimum width 96 px for the wordmark, 16 px for the mark alone.
- Do not retype it, stretch it, outline it, add shadows or recolour it. Do not put a gold "hire" or a bare gold core on a light background.

## Colour

| Name | Value | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| Mist | `#E1EBF0` | Light surface |
| Teal | `#A9CDD4` | Soft fills, the mark on dark |
| Steel | `#477A91` | The mark and the AI on light |
| Deep steel | `#2E5C72` | Buttons and links on light |
| Ink | `#17313E` | Text on light |
| Gold | `#D6AA58` | The AI on dark, the dot, the action colour on dark |
| Night | `#0F2029` | Dark surface |

Contrast, measured: gold on white is 2.15 to 1, so it is never used for small text on light. Gold on night is 7.74 to 1. Deep steel on white is 7.27 to 1. See the [design system](design-system.md) for every pair.

## Voice

Dry wit and a stoic backbone. Say the number, what it means, and one line worth keeping. Never "don't worry".

- Sounds like hireframe: "A rejection is mail. Not a verdict." "26 applications is a small sample. Treat every comparison as a hint, not a result."
- Does not: "Don't worry, the right role is out there!" "Supercharge your career with AI."
- Plain words, short sentences, no hype. Write for someone who reads English as a second language.

## Words to use

Each is true of the product today. Note the two layers of privacy: the dashboard and your files are local, and what you give Claude is processed under your Claude account's settings. See [privacy](privacy.md).

- **Tagline:** Find a job. Keep your head.
- **One line:** hireframe is the AI job search kit for designers. Open source. It keeps an honest record, shows your real odds, and keeps your files on your computer.
- **Short description:** hireframe, the AI job search kit for designers, helps you run a calm, honest job search with Claude. No code: everything is plain text, the dashboard runs in your browser, and your files stay yours.
- **Bio (LinkedIn, X, GitHub):** hireframe: the AI job search kit for designers. Open source, built on Claude, made by a UX designer during a real search. Honest numbers, your files on your computer.
- **Repository description:** The AI job search kit for designers, built on Claude. No code, honest numbers, a local dashboard, your files stay on your computer.
